Hi, I'm Sam!

  • Certified Personal Trainer
  • Nutrition Coach
  • Professional Dancer

I'm a certified personal trainer, nutrition coach, and professional dancer with experience working with clients of all ages and fitness levels. Over the years, I've helped people build healthier lifestyles through strength training, better nutrition habits, accountability, and realistic routines they can actually stick with. I believe fitness should make you feel stronger, more confident, and more capable, not overwhelmed or like you have to be perfect.

After getting engaged myself, I realized just how much goes into this season of life. Between wedding planning, work, appointments, events, and everything else, taking care of yourself can easily get pushed to the bottom of the list.

That's why I created The Fit Fiancé. I wanted to build a platform that gives brides a place to start, a plan to follow, and the accountability to keep going. No crash diets or unrealistic expectations. Just guidance, healthy habits, strength training, nutrition, and a little extra motivation along the way.
